Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

The showcase

The demo uses fictional drafts. Replacement text and sample edits stay in browser memory. The showcase code adds no cookies or analytics. Vercel hosts the site and may process request information such as IP address and browser details. Vercel privacy notice

If you connect Buffer

The separate Patchwork app requests account:read, posts:read and posts:write through Buffer OAuth. The app reads account, organization, channel and eligible draft details so you can select drafts and review edits. It sends approved draft edits to Buffer when you apply them. Buffer privacy policy

The app uses an HTTP-only session cookie and stores the Buffer access token encrypted on the server. A connected session lasts up to one hour. The instance’s SQLite database holds draft copies, linked phrases, reviews and receipts. A campaign expires seven days after its last saved change. The app clears expired records when a workspace is opened.

Remove campaign data

Use Clear campaign to remove draft copies, links, reviews and receipts from Patchwork. Disconnect ends the session and deletes its stored token. You can revoke Patchwork’s access in Buffer. If a write has an unknown outcome, Patchwork retains the organization, post and operation identifiers to prevent a duplicate edit. Those identifiers have no automatic expiry.

Questions

For questions that contain no private information, use the public issue tracker. Arrange a verified private channel with the maintainer before sharing credentials, draft text or other private details. Security reporting guidance
